Raise an AI from nothing.

Mnemo grows a brand-new language model on your own computer. No borrowed brains, no cloud. You choose how big it starts and how much it can remember. It reads, it grows, it learns to talk.

It knows nothing.

Random weights. No vocabulary. Mnemo builds its own tokenizer from the first text it reads, then starts guessing the next word, badly.

01 · first words

Shapes of language.

After a few minutes of reading, the gibberish gets punctuation, real words, the rhythm of sentences.

02 · conversation course

It learns to talk.

One click runs thousands of everyday conversations. Eight minutes later it says hi back, tells jokes, and knows its own name.

03 · it grows

Then it gets bigger.

When it has learned enough for its size, Mnemo grows wider and deeper without forgetting anything: 12M → 99M → 545M → 1B+, as far as your RAM allows.

Honest answers.

    
How smart will it get?
It starts out writing gibberish. After the conversation course it handles small
      talk. With days of reading it writes real sentences about what it studied. It won't match ChatGPT or Claude, which train
      on thousands of GPUs. Mnemo is about owning and watching the whole thing.

    
Why a command instead of a download?
Downloaded apps need a paid Apple certificate, or macOS
      calls them "damaged". The installer builds Mnemo on your own computer, so it just works.

    
What do I need?
Mac with Apple chip (8 GB works, 16 GB+ is better), Windows 10/11, or Linux.
      An NVIDIA GPU makes it much faster. Keep 5–20 GB free for its reading library.

    
Why do I need an account?
It keeps Mnemo yours across updates and is how future
      features (sharing models, cloud training) will work. Your model and data still never leave your computer.

    
Is it free?
Yes, free to use. The source is public so you can see exactly what runs on your machine, but it may not be redistributed or sold. Pro extras may come later. Sign in and join the waitlist.
